How broadcast works
When you generate and sync lessons in the platform, they are stored under your admin household. Broadcasting copies them to every active paying family — so their platform shows your content when they click Open Deck or Open Workbook.

Run broadcast after every generation session. Trial and active families both receive content. Expired households are excluded.
